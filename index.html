❓ FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?
Go to Settings (gear icon) → tap "Restore Purchases". Your Premium access will be restored instantly.


How do I change the language?
Go to Settings → Language → select from 19 available languages. All quotes and interface text will update immediately.


How do I enable or disable categories?
Go to Settings → Categories → toggle the categories you want on or off. Premium unlocks all 7 categories.


How does auto-save work?
Set your preferred save time in Settings → Reflections → Save Time. Your journal entry will be automatically saved at that time and locked for the day.


Can I sync across devices?
Yes! Enable iCloud Sync in Settings to sync your journal, goals, and preferences across all your Apple devices. (Premium feature)


How do I export my journal?
Go to History → tap the share icon (top left) → choose "Export as Text" or "Export as PDF". (Premium feature)


Is my data private?
Yes. Morniva does not collect any personal data. Everything is stored locally on your device or in your private iCloud account. See our Privacy Policy.
