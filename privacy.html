Privacy Policy

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Morniva is designed with privacy at its core. We believe your journal, reflections, and personal data belong to you and only you.

No Data Collection

Morniva does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your reflections, mood entries, goals, and preferences are stored locally on your device and optionally in your private iCloud account.

No Analytics

We do not use any analytics frameworks, crash reporting tools, or tracking SDKs. We have no way to know how you use the app, what you write in your journal, or what quotes you read.

No Third-Party Services

Morniva does not integrate with any third-party services. The only external communications are:

• Apple StoreKit — for processing in-app purchases, handled entirely by Apple.
• Apple iCloud — for optional sync of your data across your devices, handled entirely by Apple. This is opt-in and can be disabled at any time in Settings.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud Sync in Settings, your reflections, goals, and preferences are synced across your Apple devices using Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage. This data is encrypted by Apple and is only accessible to you through your Apple ID. We have no access to your iCloud data.

In-App Purchases

Morniva offers a one-time premium purchase processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. All transaction handling is managed by Apple.

Data Export

Morniva allows you to export your journal as a PDF or text file. This export is generated locally on your device and shared only through the method you choose (AirDrop, email, Files, etc.). We never see or store your exported data.

Children's Privacy

Morniva does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app is safe for all ages and is rated 4+.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, changes will be reflected on this page. Given that we collect no data, we do not anticipate any significant changes.

Contact
